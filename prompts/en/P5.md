# P5 — Whig-history / teleology self-check

> Verbatim (source: `附录_数学思想史工具与指令集.md` §6). English rendering of the Chinese prompt.
> Use when: a section is drafted and you want the "Whig history check" run on it.
> Grade note: no letter grades in this prompt.

```
Run a "Whig history check" on the text below. Find faults only; do not praise.

Text:
"""
{{paragraph}}
"""

Check the six symptoms one by one. When one hits, quote the sentence and give a rewrite:

1. Teleology: "inevitable", "destined", "paved the way for", "already contained".
2. Anachronism: describing past objects with later terms (calling a 17th-century procedure a "limit").
3. Victory narrative: only the winning route, with no competing option that was equally reasonable then.
4. Motive attribution: writing "they already saw its use" because the result turned out useful.
5. Smoothing: dropping the errors, regressions and reversals of the time.
6. Isolation: taking a concept out of the dispute it belonged to.

Output: symptom | sentence | problem | suggested rewrite (keep the meaning, change only the narrative frame)
End with one line: is this text closer to "history" or to "a textbook"?
```
